# FirstPaying — Product Context

## What This Is
FirstPaying is a micro-SaaS tool for solo founders and indie 
hackers who have built a product but can't get their first 
paying customer. They paste their landing page URL or describe 
their product, and get three AI-generated outputs instantly.

## The One Screen
Single page app. No dashboard. No navigation. No settings.
One input. One analyse button. Three output sections.

## The Three Outputs
1. Landing page weaknesses — 5 specific bullet points explaining
   why the page isn't converting
2. Reddit launch post — ready to copy-paste, written in authentic
   founder voice for r/SaaS or r/indiehackers
3. Cold outreach email sequence — 3 emails, under 100 words each,
   conversational tone, specific subject lines

## The Paywall
Free users get 2 analyses. After that, show upgrade modal.
Paid users ($19/month via Stripe) get unlimited analyses.
Track usage per user in Supabase.

## The Buyer
Solo founder, age 19-32, global audience, pays in USD.
They are frustrated, not skeptical. They will pay immediately
if the output is genuinely useful.

## What This Is NOT
Not a dashboard. Not an analytics tool. Not a marketing suite.
Not a team product. No settings page. No history view (v1).
No integrations. Ship the smallest thing that delivers the 3 outputs.
